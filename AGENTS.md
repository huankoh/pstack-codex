# pstack-codex change policy

Maintain a faithful original-pstack baseline with the pinned pstack-claude updates.
Preserve upstream workflow scope, procedures, roles, panels, review requirements,
and helper behavior. Limit the port to required Codex and local-worktree compatibility.

Before any change beyond that faithful baseline, present the proposed change and
its rationale to the user and obtain their explicit approval. Record the approval
with the change. Do not treat a general request to maintain or validate the plugin
as approval for workflow simplification, omitted capabilities, new defaults, or
additional fixes. Keep unapproved proposals out of the active package.

Retain source provenance and run the fidelity check after editing imported files.
